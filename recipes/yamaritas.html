Description

I trust you've heard of French Fries? This dish takes that concept to the next level.

Yamaritas are, in essence, strips of yam drenched and lathered in a mix of eggs and flour, seasoned to preference and fried to perfection.

Ingredients

  • Yam
  • Eggs
  • Flour
  • Flavors, Seasoning, and Spices
    • Salt,
    • Onions,
    • Broth,
    • Peppers,
    • and some sort of Sauce to cherry the icing

Steps

  1. Cut the yam
  2. Boil and cool the yam
  3. Mix the eggs with your favorite flavors, seasoning, and spices
  4. Dip each cut of yam into the flour, then into the egg mix
  5. Fry to perfection
  6. Enjoy!
